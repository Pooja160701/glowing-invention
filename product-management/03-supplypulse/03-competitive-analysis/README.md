# SupplyPulse — Competitive Analysis

## Purpose
This layer maps the supply-chain planning and inventory-management landscape, identifies documented competitor capabilities, and defines differentiation hypotheses for SupplyPulse.

## Competitive set
- SAP Integrated Business Planning (SAP IBP)
- Oracle NetSuite Inventory / Demand Planning
- Manhattan Active Supply Chain Planning
- Kinaxis Maestro / RapidResponse-style concurrent planning category
- Blue Yonder supply-chain planning category

> Research note: Competitor capability descriptions are based on public vendor materials reviewed for this portfolio. Vendor claims are presented as vendor claims, not independently validated performance results.

## Key market observation
The established market already includes substantial capabilities in demand planning, inventory planning, replenishment, allocation, scenario analysis, alerts, and supply-chain integration. SAP describes IBP as combining S&OP, forecasting/demand, response/supply, replenishment, inventory planning, analytics, simulations, and alerts. Oracle documents demand planning and supply planning tied to inventory management and reorder decisions. ## Strategic implication
SupplyPulse should not position itself as simply another forecasting dashboard. Its differentiation hypothesis is a focused operational decision layer:
trusted data → prioritized exceptions → explainable recommendation → human approval → tracked resolution.
