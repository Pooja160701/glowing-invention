# SupplyPulse Competitive Gap Hypotheses

Gap means a potential positioning opportunity to investigate, not a verified competitor weakness.

## Gap 1 — Cross-system exception context
Hypothesis: users may need a single exception record combining inventory, demand, PO, supplier, and lead-time context.
Test: prototype an exception workspace and measure task completion time.

## Gap 2 — Data trust at the decision point
Hypothesis: planners may benefit more from freshness/source indicators embedded in workflows than from a separate data-quality dashboard.
Test: compare decision confidence with and without provenance indicators.

## Gap 3 — Explainability for operational approval
Hypothesis: recommendations should answer "why this quantity, why now, and what changed?"
Test: concept-test recommendation cards with adjustable inputs.

## Gap 4 — Human approval as a product primitive
Hypothesis: operational teams may prefer configurable approval boundaries based on value, criticality, or risk.
Test: evaluate approval policies across low-, medium-, and high-impact actions.

## Gap 5 — Resolution evidence
Hypothesis: capturing owner, decision, action, and resolution reason can improve organizational learning.
Test: shadow an exception-management workflow and measure handoff loss.

## Gap 6 — Lightweight integration layer
Hypothesis: some organizations may prefer a focused decision layer that works with current systems rather than a replacement project.
Test: interview buyers about integration constraints, implementation duration, and system-of-record boundaries.
