## Kinaxis / Blue Yonder
These competitors are included as market categories in this portfolio layer. Before using a detailed capability matrix in an external business document, verify current product packaging and feature claims directly from their current official product materials.

## Competitive research discipline
1. Date-stamp competitive research.
2. Prefer primary vendor sources for capability claims.
3. Separate documented capability from inference.
4. Never claim a competitor lacks a feature without reliable evidence.
5. Revalidate before external/customer-facing use because enterprise software changes frequently.
