# SupplyPulse Win/Loss Hypotheses

These are research hypotheses, not predictions or claims of market outcome.

| Scenario | Possible reason to select SupplyPulse | Possible reason not to select SupplyPulse | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existing ERP is adequate | Faster exception workflow across systems | Existing platform may already cover the workflow | Workflow comparison |
| Planning suite is complex | Focused operational layer | Additional platform may increase complexity | Buyer interview |
| Data is fragmented | Cross-system context | Integration effort may be too high | Technical discovery |
| Teams distrust automation | Human approval + explanation | Manual approval may reduce automation value | Concept test |
| Exception volume is high | Prioritized queue | Risk scoring may be perceived as opaque | Prototype test |
| Supplier risk is volatile | Connected PO/inventory risk | Supplier data may be incomplete | Data audit |
| Leadership needs visibility | Shared KPI + drill-down | Existing BI may be sufficient | Reporting workflow study |

## Decision criteria to validate
- time to first value,
- integration effort,
- reduction in manual reconciliation,
- exception resolution time,
- decision confidence,
- service-risk visibility,
- user adoption,
- governance requirements,
- total cost of ownership.
