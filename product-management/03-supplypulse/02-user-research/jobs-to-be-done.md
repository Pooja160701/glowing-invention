# SupplyPulse Jobs-to-be-Done

> JTBD statements are hypotheses derived from synthetic research and require validation with real users.

## Core functional jobs

| JTBD ID | Job statement | Trigger | Desired outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| JTBD-001 | When starting a planning cycle, I want to understand inventory health by SKU/location so I can focus attention where it matters. | Daily/weekly review | Trusted inventory picture |
| JTBD-002 | When service risk is emerging, I want to detect likely stockouts early so I can take corrective action before customer impact. | Demand/supply change | Earlier intervention |
| JTBD-003 | When inventory is excessive or aging, I want to identify the value and cause so I can reduce avoidable working capital. | Aging threshold | Actionable excess view |
| JTBD-004 | When a replenishment decision is required, I want demand, inventory, lead time, and safety-stock context together so I can make an informed decision. | Reorder trigger | Faster confident decision |
| JTBD-005 | When a recommendation is presented, I want to understand why it was generated so I can approve or reject it responsibly. | Recommendation | Explainable decision |
| JTBD-006 | When supplier performance changes, I want to see the operational impact so I can intervene before service is affected. | Late PO/lead-time change | Proactive supplier action |
| JTBD-007 | When inventory data conflicts, I want to identify the source and freshness so I can resolve the discrepancy. | Reconciliation issue | Restored trust |
| JTBD-008 | When many exceptions occur, I want them prioritized by risk and business impact so I can work the highest-value issues first. | Exception spike | Reduced decision latency |
| JTBD-009 | When leadership asks what changed, I want consistent metrics and drill-down evidence so I can explain the situation. | Review meeting | Faster reporting |
| JTBD-010 | When handing work to another team, I want an owner and next action recorded so exceptions do not disappear between functions. | Cross-functional handoff | Closed-loop execution |

## Emotional jobs
- Feel confident that the data reflects reality.
- Avoid being surprised by shortages or supplier failures.
- Know why the system recommends an action.
- Feel in control of high-impact decisions.
- Avoid spending the day reconciling reports.

## Social jobs
- Explain decisions credibly to managers.
- Demonstrate control of supply risk.
- Coordinate across planning, procurement, warehouse, and leadership.
- Provide an auditable rationale for material decisions.

## Product implication
SupplyPulse should optimize for **decision quality and speed**, not merely dashboard consumption.
