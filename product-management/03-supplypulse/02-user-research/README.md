# SupplyPulse — User Research

## Purpose
This layer translates the SupplyPulse product strategy into evidence-shaped research artifacts covering inventory planners, procurement managers, warehouse/operations managers, and supply-chain leaders.

> **Research status: SYNTHETIC / PORTFOLIO DATA**
>
> No real users, customers, companies, or confidential operational data were interviewed for this portfolio project. The participant quotes, observations, frequencies, and findings in this folder are realistic synthetic examples created to demonstrate a professional product-research workflow. They must not be presented as real customer evidence.

## Research objectives
1. Understand how supply-chain teams monitor inventory health.
2. Identify how teams detect and prioritize stockout, excess, supplier, and data-quality risk.
3. Understand current replenishment decision workflows and approval points.
4. Identify trust barriers around forecasts, recommendations, and source data.
5. Understand the operational impact of fragmented systems and spreadsheet-based work.
6. Generate hypotheses for PRD requirements and later validation.

## Research questions
- What information must planners see before taking action?
- Which exceptions are most costly or time-sensitive?
- How do teams determine whether inventory data is trustworthy?
- What causes replenishment decisions to be delayed?
- What makes a recommendation explainable enough to approve?
- Where do supplier lead-time changes surface today?
- Which tasks are repetitive enough to automate safely?
- What evidence do leaders need to understand service and working-capital risk?
