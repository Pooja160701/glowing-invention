# SupplyPulse Epic Breakdown

## EP-01 — Inventory Visibility
Goal: establish a trusted operational view of inventory position.

Stories: US-001 to US-004

## EP-02 — Demand & Supply Intelligence
Goal: provide the signals required to understand future inventory exposure.

Stories: US-005 to US-008

## EP-03 — Exception Management
Goal: turn supply-chain risk into prioritized, owned, actionable work.

Stories: US-009 to US-017

## EP-04 — Replenishment Decision Support
Goal: help users make explainable replenishment decisions while retaining human control.

Stories: US-018 to US-024

## EP-05 — Supplier Risk
Goal: connect supplier performance and PO changes to operational inventory exposure.

Stories: US-025 to US-028

## EP-06 — Data Quality & Lineage
Goal: make data trust visible and prevent degraded inputs from silently driving decisions.

Stories: US-029 to US-032

## EP-07 — Governance & Audit
Goal: enforce access boundaries and preserve material decision history.

Stories: US-033 to US-035

## EP-08 — Analytics & Reporting
Goal: connect operational exceptions to executive-level inventory and service outcomes.

Stories: US-036 to US-037

## EP-09 — Integrations
Goal: ingest source data reliably and expose integration health.

Stories: US-038 to US-039

## EP-10 — Administration
Goal: allow controlled configuration of operational rules.

Stories: US-040

## Suggested implementation sequence
1. Identity + canonical data model
2. Inventory ingestion and visibility
3. Demand/supply context
4. Exception engine
5. Exception workflow
6. Replenishment recommendation workflow
7. Supplier risk
8. Data quality and lineage
9. Analytics
10. Administration
