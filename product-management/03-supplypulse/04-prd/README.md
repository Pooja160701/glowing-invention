# SupplyPulse — Product Requirements Document

## Product
SupplyPulse — Intelligent Supply Chain & Inventory Platform

## Purpose
Translate the product strategy, synthetic user research, and competitive analysis into an implementation-ready MVP requirements baseline.

> Evidence note: User-research inputs in this portfolio are synthetic. Requirements remain hypotheses until validated with real customers and production data.

## Product promise
Know what you have → Understand what is changing → Anticipate what is at risk → Act before service is impacted

## Primary outcome
Reduce avoidable inventory and supply exceptions and shorten the time required to understand, decide, and resolve operational risk.

## MVP scope
1. Inventory visibility
2. Demand and supply signals
3. Exception management
4. Replenishment decision support
5. Supplier risk visibility
6. Auditability and data-quality visibility
7. Operational analytics

## Explicit non-goals
- ERP replacement
- Autonomous procurement
- Full production scheduling optimization
- Autonomous high-impact purchasing
- Advanced multi-echelon optimization in MVP
- Replacing an organization's existing forecast system

## Success metrics
North-star candidate: Resolved High-Impact Exception Rate (RHEIR) — percentage of high-impact exceptions resolved within their defined service window, with a recorded owner and resolution state.

Supporting metrics:
- exception detection lead time
- median exception resolution time
- replenishment decision cycle time
- recommendation approval rate
- manual reconciliation time
- data-quality exception rate
- stockout-risk exposure
- excess-inventory exposure
- active-user rate
