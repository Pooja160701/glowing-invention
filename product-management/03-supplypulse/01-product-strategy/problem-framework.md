# SupplyPulse — Problem Framework

## Problem statement

Supply-chain teams need to make inventory and replenishment decisions using information spread across multiple systems, but current workflows can make it difficult to identify the most urgent exceptions, understand their causes, and act quickly with confidence.

## Jobs to be supported

### Inventory health
Understand which SKUs and locations require attention.

### Service protection
Identify inventory conditions that could create stockout risk.

### Working-capital control
Identify excess and aging inventory.

### Replenishment
Understand what should be reordered and why.

### Supplier management
Identify lead-time and fulfillment deviations.

### Operational response
Resolve exceptions with ownership and traceability.

## Outcome hypothesis

If supply-chain teams receive a trusted, prioritized, explainable operational view, then they can reduce avoidable exception resolution time and improve inventory decision quality.

This requires validation through user research.
