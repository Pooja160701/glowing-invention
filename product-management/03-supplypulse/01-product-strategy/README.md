# SupplyPulse — Product Strategy

**Status:** Portfolio-ready specification  
**Project:** SupplyPulse — Intelligent Supply Chain & Inventory Platform

SupplyPulse is a supply-chain visibility and inventory optimization platform designed to help organizations understand inventory health, anticipate supply disruptions, and coordinate replenishment decisions.

## Product promise

**Know what you have → Understand what is changing → Anticipate what is at risk → Act before service is impacted**

## Strategic focus

The MVP focuses on inventory visibility, demand/supply signals, replenishment workflows, exception management, and operational decision support.

SupplyPulse is not intended to replace ERP systems. It is a decision-support and workflow layer that can integrate with operational systems.
