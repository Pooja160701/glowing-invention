# SupplyPulse — Supply Chain Journey

## Core journey

**Plan → Source → Receive → Store → Monitor → Replenish → Review**

### 1. Plan
Supply-chain teams review demand, forecast, inventory targets, and service expectations.

### 2. Source
Procurement manages suppliers and purchase orders.

### 3. Receive
Inbound inventory is expected and received into locations.

### 4. Store
Inventory is held across warehouses or locations.

### 5. Monitor
Teams monitor inventory position, aging, demand, supply, and exceptions.

### 6. Replenish
Users review replenishment recommendations and approve actions.

### 7. Review
Leaders review service, inventory, working capital, supplier, and exception metrics.

## Primary friction points

- Fragmented inventory data.
- Delayed visibility into supply disruption.
- Manual spreadsheet analysis.
- Unclear replenishment rationale.
- Poor exception prioritization.
- Supplier performance variability.
- Data-quality inconsistencies.

## Product opportunity

SupplyPulse should shorten the distance between **signal → explanation → decision → action**.
