# SupplyPulse — Product Strategy

## Strategic problem

Supply-chain teams often work across ERP, WMS, procurement, spreadsheets, supplier portals, and reporting tools. This can create fragmented visibility and delayed response to inventory and supply exceptions.

SupplyPulse focuses on connecting those signals into a common operational view.

## Strategic pillars

### 1. Inventory Visibility
Create a trusted view of inventory position, availability, movement, and aging.

### 2. Demand & Supply Intelligence
Combine historical demand, forecasts, lead times, and supply signals.

### 3. Exception Management
Prioritize stockout risk, excess inventory, late supply, and unusual demand/supply behavior.

### 4. Replenishment Decision Support
Provide explainable reorder recommendations and allow users to review and approve actions.

### 5. Supplier Risk Visibility
Track lead-time behavior, fulfillment reliability, and supplier-related exceptions.

### 6. Measurement & Governance
Provide traceable metrics, data-quality monitoring, and decision history.

## Strategic differentiation hypothesis

SupplyPulse can differentiate by combining:
- Exception-first workflows.
- Explainable recommendations.
- Inventory + demand + supplier context in one decision view.
- Human approval for material replenishment actions.
- Strong data-quality and lineage visibility.

This is a product hypothesis to validate through research and competitive analysis.

## Success definition

The product should reduce avoidable inventory exceptions and decision latency while preserving inventory data trust and operational control.
