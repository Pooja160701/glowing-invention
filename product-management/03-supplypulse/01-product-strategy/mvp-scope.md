# SupplyPulse — MVP Scope

## MVP objective

Give supply-chain teams one operational workspace for inventory health, supply/demand exceptions, and replenishment decisions.

## P0 — Inventory visibility

- Inventory by SKU and location.
- On-hand quantity.
- Available quantity.
- Reserved quantity.
- In-transit quantity.
- Inventory value.
- Stock aging indicators.
- Inventory status.

## P0 — Demand and supply signals

- Historical demand.
- Forecast input.
- Supplier lead time.
- Purchase order status.
- Expected receipts.
- Demand/supply variance.

## P0 — Exception management

- Stockout risk.
- Excess inventory.
- Late purchase order.
- Supplier lead-time deviation.
- Demand anomaly.
- Inventory data-quality exception.
- Prioritized exception queue.

## P0 — Replenishment decision support

- Reorder point calculation.
- Suggested reorder quantity.
- Safety-stock visibility.
- Lead-time context.
- Recommendation explanation.
- Approve/reject recommendation.
- Decision audit trail.

## P0 — Supplier visibility

- Supplier performance summary.
- On-time delivery rate.
- Average lead time.
- Lead-time variability.
- Open supplier exceptions.

## P0 — Analytics

- Inventory health dashboard.
- Service-risk dashboard.
- Exception dashboard.
- Replenishment decision metrics.

## P1 — Later

- Multi-echelon optimization.
- Automated purchase-order creation.
- Advanced demand forecasting.
- Scenario simulation.
- Supplier collaboration portal.
- Autonomous replenishment.

## Explicit non-goals

- Replacing the ERP.
- Fully autonomous procurement.
- Financial accounting.
- Contract/legal management.
- Production scheduling optimization.
- Human-resource workflows.
