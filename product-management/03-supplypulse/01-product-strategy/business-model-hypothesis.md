# SupplyPulse — Business Model Hypothesis

**Status:** Hypothesis for validation

## Target buyer

- Supply-chain organizations.
- Retailers.
- Distributors.
- Manufacturers.
- Multi-location businesses.

## Potential buyer roles

- VP / Head of Supply Chain.
- Supply Chain Director.
- Inventory Planning Manager.
- Procurement Leader.
- Operations Leader.

## Model hypothesis

**B2B SaaS**

Potential pricing dimensions:
- Number of locations.
- Number of SKUs.
- Monthly transaction volume.
- Number of users.
- Advanced analytics / optimization tier.

## Value hypothesis

Customers may pay for:
- Reduced stockout exposure.
- Reduced excess inventory.
- Faster exception resolution.
- Better planner productivity.
- Better supplier visibility.
- More explainable replenishment decisions.

## Important validation questions

- Which supply-chain problem has the highest economic value?
- Who owns the budget?
- Is inventory optimization or visibility the initial buying trigger?
- What integrations are required for adoption?
- How much automation will customers trust?
- Which metric best demonstrates ROI?
