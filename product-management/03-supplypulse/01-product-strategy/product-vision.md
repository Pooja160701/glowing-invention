# SupplyPulse — Product Vision

## Vision

Help supply-chain teams turn fragmented inventory and demand data into timely, explainable decisions.

## Mission

Build a practical supply-chain intelligence layer that connects inventory visibility, demand signals, supplier risk, replenishment workflows, and operational exceptions.

## Product promise

SupplyPulse helps teams answer four questions:

1. What inventory do we have?
2. What is likely to change?
3. What is at risk?
4. What action should happen next?

## Target users

### Inventory Planner
Owns inventory health, replenishment decisions, and stock availability.

### Procurement Manager
Monitors supplier performance, purchase orders, lead times, and sourcing risk.

### Warehouse / Operations Manager
Needs accurate inventory visibility and exception handling.

### Supply Chain Leader
Needs portfolio-level visibility into service, inventory, working capital, and operational risk.

### Business Stakeholder
Needs understandable reporting without operating the underlying supply-chain systems.

## Product principles

1. **Visibility before optimization** — bad data cannot produce trustworthy recommendations.
2. **Explainable decisions** — users should understand why an item is flagged.
3. **Exception-first workflows** — surface the items that need attention.
4. **Human-in-the-loop** — recommendations support decisions; authorized users remain accountable.
5. **Operational simplicity** — prioritize actionable workflows over dashboard complexity.
6. **Data lineage matters** — every important metric should have a traceable source.
7. **Integration-friendly** — coexist with ERP, WMS, procurement, and planning systems.
