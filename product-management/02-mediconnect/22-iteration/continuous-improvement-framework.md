# Continuous Improvement Framework

## 1. Observe

Collect:
- KPI movement.
- Funnel changes.
- Support themes.
- Research evidence.
- Operational exceptions.
- Experiment results.

## 2. Diagnose

Ask:
- What changed?
- Where did it change?
- Which users/workflows are affected?
- Is the signal statistically or operationally meaningful?
- Could measurement quality explain the change?

## 3. Decide

Classify the finding:

### Optimize
Existing workflow can be improved.

### Build
Validated product capability is missing.

### Experiment
Multiple plausible solutions exist.

### Research
The problem is not understood well enough.

### Fix
A reliability, security, privacy, or correctness issue requires remediation.

### Monitor
Evidence is insufficient for action.

### Stop / Deprioritize
Evidence or strategy no longer supports continued investment.

## 4. Execute

Create:
- User stories.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Design work.
- Engineering tasks.
- Analytics requirements.
- QA coverage.

## 5. Measure

Connect the change to:
- Primary outcome.
- Guardrails.
- Diagnostic metrics.

## 6. Learn

Record:
- Result.
- Unexpected behavior.
- New assumptions.
- Decision.
- Follow-up.

## 7. Update strategy

Material learning should update:
- Product strategy.
- Roadmap.
- Research priorities.
- Productboard/Aha!/Notion records.
- Delivery backlog.
