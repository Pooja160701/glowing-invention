# Weekly Iteration Review

## 1. Product health

Review:
- CCJR.
- Booking success.
- Search-to-book.
- Self-service change completion.
- Notification delivery.
- Exception resolution.
- Reliability.
- Security/privacy guardrails.

## 2. Customer evidence

Review:
- Patient feedback.
- Staff feedback.
- Provider feedback.
- Support themes.
- Research findings.

## 3. Experimentation

Review:
- Running experiments.
- Completed experiments.
- Inconclusive experiments.
- Decisions required.

## 4. Delivery

Review:
- Completed work.
- Blocked work.
- Defects.
- Technical debt.
- Dependencies.

## 5. Decisions

For every material signal:
- Fix.
- Optimize.
- Build.
- Experiment.
- Research.
- Monitor.
- Deprioritize.

## 6. Roadmap

Ask:
- What should move into Now?
- What should remain Next?
- What should move to Later?
- What should be removed?

## 7. Follow-up

Every action needs:
- Owner.
- Due date.
- Metric.
- Evidence link.
