# Roadmap Update Process

## Trigger conditions

Review the roadmap when:
- A KPI materially changes.
- A critical guardrail breaches its threshold.
- A major experiment produces actionable evidence.
- Repeated customer evidence identifies a new opportunity.
- A security/privacy issue changes product requirements.
- Operational burden changes materially.
- Strategic assumptions change.

## Update process

### Step 1 — Evidence packet
Collect:
- Metric evidence.
- Research evidence.
- Feedback.
- Experiment results.
- Operational impact.

### Step 2 — Product decision
Record:
- Problem.
- Evidence.
- Options.
- Decision.
- Rationale.
- Trade-offs.

### Step 3 — Planning update
Update:
- Now / Next / Later.
- Productboard feature status.
- Aha! initiative/feature status.
- Jira/Linear backlog.
- Asana/Trello delivery plans.
- Notion Product HQ.

### Step 4 — Measurement
Define the metric that will tell the team whether the roadmap change worked.

## Governance

Do not change roadmap priority solely because of a single anecdote unless it represents a critical safety, security, privacy, or correctness issue.
