Artifacts:
- Product strategy.
- User research.
- Competitive analysis.

## Definition
Artifacts:
- PRD.
- Requirements.
- User stories.
- Acceptance criteria.

## Planning
Artifacts:
- Roadmap.
- Jira.
- Confluence.
- Linear.
- Productboard.
- Aha!
- Notion.
- Trello.
- Asana.

## Design & Technical
Artifacts:
- UX flows.
- Prototype.
- Architecture.
- API documentation.

## Delivery
Artifacts:
- Development plan.
- QA.
- Release readiness.

## Measurement
Artifacts:
- Analytics instrumentation.
- Experimentation.
- BI dashboards.
- KPI framework.

## Launch
Artifacts:
- GTM.
- Pilot.
- Support readiness.
- Launch metrics.

## Learning
Artifacts:
- Feedback.
- Customer insights.
- Post-launch review.

## Iteration
Artifacts:
- Learning log.
- Iteration backlog.
- Product decisions.
- Roadmap updates.

## Loop

Iteration feeds the next discovery cycle.

That makes MediConnect a complete portfolio example of an evidence-driven product operating lifecycle rather than a collection of disconnected documents.
