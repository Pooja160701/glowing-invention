# MediConnect — Iteration & Continuous Improvement

**Status:** Portfolio-ready specification

This is the final layer of the MediConnect product lifecycle.

## Continuous product loop

Discovery
→ Problem Definition
→ Research
→ Strategy
→ PRD
→ Delivery
→ Analytics
→ Experimentation
→ Launch
→ Metrics
→ Feedback
→ Iteration
→ Discovery

The product lifecycle is intentionally circular rather than ending at launch.

## Inputs to iteration

- Product metrics.
- Customer feedback.
- Support trends.
- Research findings.
- Experiment results.
- Operational exceptions.
- Security/privacy findings.
- Reliability incidents.
- Delivery learnings.
- Strategic changes.

## Output

A refreshed, evidence-based:
- Product backlog.
- Experiment backlog.
- Research backlog.
- Roadmap.
- Product decisions.
- Technical improvement plan.
