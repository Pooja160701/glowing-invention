# Asana Operating Cadence

## Daily
- Review blocked tasks.
- Check critical work aging.
- Update task status.
- Surface dependency changes.

## Weekly product review
Review:
- CCJR
- Booking success
- Search-to-book conversion
- Availability reliability
- Notification delivery
- Self-service change rate
- Exception resolution time
- Core service reliability

Then:
1. Review milestone progress.
2. Review high-risk dependencies.
3. Make or record decisions.
4. Reprioritize backlog if evidence changed.
5. Assign owners and due dates.

## Sprint / cycle
- Planning.
- Backlog refinement.
- Delivery.
- QA.
- Review.
- Retrospective.

## Launch cadence
Before a launch milestone:
- Product readiness.
- Engineering readiness.
- Security/privacy readiness.
- Analytics readiness.
- Support readiness.
- Rollback readiness.

After launch:
- Monitor metrics.
- Review incidents.
- Synthesize feedback.
- Convert validated learnings into backlog items.
