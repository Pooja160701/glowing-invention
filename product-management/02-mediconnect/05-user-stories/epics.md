# MediConnect Epics

## E01 — Patient Account & Access

**Outcome:** Patients can securely enter and manage the platform.

Stories:
- Account registration/sign-in.
- Session management.
- Communication preferences.
- Account recovery.

## E02 — Provider Discovery

**Outcome:** Patients can identify relevant providers and clinics.

Stories:
- Search.
- Filter.
- Provider/clinic profile.
- Location and service information.

## E03 — Availability & Scheduling

**Outcome:** Patients can see valid appointment options.

Stories:
- Availability search.
- Slot freshness.
- Time-zone handling.
- Schedule conflict prevention.

## E04 — Appointment Booking

**Outcome:** Patients can successfully create appointments.

Stories:
- Select slot.
- Confirm booking.
- Prevent duplicate booking.
- Booking confirmation.

## E05 — Appointment Management

**Outcome:** Patients can manage existing appointments without unnecessary staff intervention.

Stories:
- View appointment.
- Cancel.
- Reschedule.
- View history.

## E06 — Patient Communications

**Outcome:** Patients receive useful, privacy-aware appointment communications.

Stories:
- Confirmation.
- Reminder scheduling.
- Delivery status.
- Communication preferences.

## E07 — Staff Operations

**Outcome:** Clinic teams can manage schedules and appointment workflows.

Stories:
- Staff calendar.
- Availability management.
- Appointment management.
- Provider schedule changes.

## E08 — Exception Management

**Outcome:** Operational problems become visible and recoverable.

Stories:
- Exception creation.
- Exception queue.
- Assignment.
- Resolution.
- Notification failures.

## E09 — Security, Privacy & Governance

**Outcome:** Sensitive administrative data is appropriately protected.

Stories:
- RBAC.
- Authorization.
- Audit logging.
- Data minimization.
- Security monitoring.

## E10 — Analytics & Product Operations

**Outcome:** Product and operations teams can measure the administrative journey.

Stories:
- Event instrumentation.
- Booking funnel.
- Operational metrics.
- Reliability metrics.
- Privacy-safe analytics.
