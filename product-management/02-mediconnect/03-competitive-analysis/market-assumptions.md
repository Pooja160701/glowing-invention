# Market Assumptions & Validation Plan

**Status:** Hypotheses unless explicitly marked as documented competitor information.

## Assumption 1 — Patients value self-service scheduling

**Hypothesis:** Patients prefer booking and appointment changes without calling a clinic when reliable availability is visible.

**Validation:** Measure self-service completion and support-contact reduction in a pilot.

## Assumption 2 — Availability accuracy affects trust

**Hypothesis:** Patients lose confidence when a displayed slot cannot actually be booked.

**Validation:** Track slot mismatch/error rate and conduct post-booking interviews.

## Assumption 3 — Staff need an exception queue

**Hypothesis:** A consolidated queue for cancellations, conflicts, provider unavailability, and communication failures reduces administrative effort.

**Validation:** Time current-state workflows and compare pilot resolution time.

## Assumption 4 — Reminder preferences matter

**Hypothesis:** Patients are more likely to engage when reminder timing/channel is configurable and delivery status is visible.

**Validation:** Controlled reminder experiment with privacy-safe aggregate measurement.

## Assumption 5 — Privacy transparency influences adoption

**Hypothesis:** Clear explanation of what data is collected and why improves trust during onboarding.

**Validation:** Usability test onboarding variants; measure comprehension and drop-off.

## Assumption 6 — Clinic-side integration is a purchase/adoption dependency

**Hypothesis:** Clinics will resist a patient-facing booking product if staff must maintain availability in multiple systems.

**Validation:** Interview clinic operators and map current scheduling systems before committing to integration scope.

## Competitive research caveat

Public product pages are useful for capability discovery but do not reveal the complete internal architecture, adoption rates, pricing economics, customer satisfaction, or operational performance of competitors. Those dimensions require primary research, vendor documentation, customer research, or validated market datasets.
