## 5. Comparison framing

These competitors should not be treated as interchangeable. Zocdoc and Practo emphasize discovery/booking ecosystems; Doctolib spans booking and professional operations; Epic operates deeply inside health-system workflows.

MediConnect therefore needs a clearly defined target segment and workflow boundary rather than attempting to match every feature in every category.
