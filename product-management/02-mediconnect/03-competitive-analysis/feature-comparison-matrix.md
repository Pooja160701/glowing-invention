## Product-management interpretation

The matrix is deliberately not a scorecard. It identifies capabilities and boundaries that inform requirements.

### MVP focus

MediConnect should establish a narrow core:

**Discover → Check availability → Book → Confirm → Remind → Attend → Manage exceptions**

The MVP should avoid expanding into clinical records, diagnosis, treatment, or broad healthcare-commerce functionality.
