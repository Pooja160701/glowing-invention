# Product Metrics Review

## Daily operational review

Focus:
- Booking reliability.
- Notification delivery.
- Critical exceptions.
- Core availability.
- Security anomalies.

## Weekly product review

### 1. North Star
CCJR trend and cohort definition.

### 2. Funnel
Search → Availability → Slot → Booking.

### 3. Self-service
Cancellation and rescheduling.

### 4. Operations
Exceptions, resolution time, support themes.

### 5. Trust
Security and privacy indicators.

### 6. Experiments
Running experiments and recent decisions.

### 7. Decisions
What evidence requires a product decision?

### 8. Roadmap
What should be accelerated, changed, or investigated?

## Monthly product review

Review:
- Strategic KPI trends.
- Pilot/organization performance.
- Product-market evidence.
- Cost/operational burden.
- Major customer feedback themes.
- Roadmap assumptions.
- Technical and operational debt.

## Review principle

A metric is useful only when it can change a decision, trigger an investigation, or validate an important product assumption.
