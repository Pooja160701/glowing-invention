# Productboard Roadmap View

## NOW — Reliable access

### Initiative
Reliable Appointment Access

### Features
- Real-time availability.
- Self-service booking.
- Staff scheduling console.
- RBAC + organization isolation.
- Privacy center.
- Analytics instrumentation.

### Outcome
Patients can book valid appointments while organizations maintain safe operational control.

---

## NEXT — Self-service journey

### Initiative
Self-Service Patient Journey

### Features
- Cancellation workflow.
- Self-service rescheduling.
- Preference-aware reminders.
- Notification delivery console.
- Exception queue.

### Outcome
Routine post-booking work requires less manual intervention.

---

## LATER — Workflow expansion

### Initiative
Workflow Expansion

### Features
- Advanced scheduling.
- External scheduling integrations.

### Outcome
MediConnect can support more complex provider organizations after MVP evidence validates demand.

## Roadmap governance

Feature movement requires:
- Customer evidence.
- Clear outcome.
- Dependency review.
- Security/privacy review.
- Operational readiness.
- Measurable success criteria.
