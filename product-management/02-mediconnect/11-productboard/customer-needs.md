# Customer Needs

## CN-001 — Confidence in availability

**Persona:** Patient

**Need:** Know that a displayed appointment slot is genuinely available.

**Evidence:** Synthetic research identified availability confidence as a recurring patient theme.

**Related requirements:** R-002

**Desired outcome:** Lower slot mismatch and booking failure.

---

## CN-002 — Fast self-service booking

**Persona:** Patient

**Need:** Complete an appointment booking without calling the clinic.

**Evidence:** Synthetic research identified booking coordination and manual effort as recurring themes.

**Related requirements:** R-003

**Desired outcome:** Higher search-to-book conversion.

---

## CN-003 — Easy appointment changes

**Persona:** Patient

**Need:** Cancel or reschedule without unnecessary staff interaction.

**Evidence:** Synthetic research identified self-service changes as an opportunity.

**Related requirements:** R-006/R-007

**Desired outcome:** Higher self-service change rate.

---

## CN-004 — Reliable reminders

**Persona:** Patient

**Need:** Receive appointment reminders through permitted channels with clear communication preferences.

**Evidence:** Synthetic research identified reminder preference and delivery visibility themes.

**Related requirements:** R-005/R-008

**Desired outcome:** Higher reminder delivery rate and fewer avoidable support contacts.

## CN-007 — Privacy transparency

**Persona:** Patient / Staff / Organization Admin

**Related requirements:** R-011

## CN-008 — Operational measurement

**Persona:** Product / Operations

**Need:** Measure booking, communication, exception, and reliability performance.
