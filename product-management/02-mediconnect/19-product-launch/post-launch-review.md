# Post-Launch Product Review

## Review window

Recommended:
- 24-hour launch review.
- 7-day operational review.
- 30-day product review.

## 1. What happened?

Summarize:
- Usage.
- Booking activity.
- Reliability.
- Exceptions.
- Support.
- Incidents.

## 2. What did users experience?

Synthesize:
- Patient feedback.
- Staff feedback.
- Provider feedback.
- Support themes.

## 3. What changed?

Compare:
- Baseline metrics.
- Pilot metrics.
- Experiment results.
- Operational workload.

## 4. What surprised us?

Document:
- Unexpected behaviors.
- Edge cases.
- Data-quality problems.
- Workflow gaps.

## 5. Decisions

For each finding:
- Keep.
- Improve.
- Investigate.
- Stop.
- Defer.

## 6. Roadmap updates

Convert validated findings into:
- Product backlog items.
- Research questions.
- Experiments.
- Technical debt.
- Operational improvements.

## 7. Follow-up owners

Every material action should have:
- Owner.
- Due date.
- Success metric.
- Linked evidence.
