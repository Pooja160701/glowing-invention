# Controlled Pilot Plan

## Pilot objective

Validate product reliability, operational fit, and measurable reduction of administrative appointment friction.

## Pilot stages

### Stage 1 — Readiness
Confirm all required launch gates.

### Stage 2 — Limited workflow
Enable a narrow set of appointment workflows.

### Stage 3 — Expanded workflow
Introduce self-service changes and operational exception handling after core booking evidence is stable.

### Stage 4 — Scale decision
Review evidence before expanding organization or appointment volume.

## Pilot feedback

Collect:
- Patient usability feedback.
- Staff workflow feedback.
- Provider stakeholder feedback.
- Support themes.
- Exception categories.
- Booking failure reasons.

## Exit criteria

The pilot should have:
- Reliable core booking.
- No unresolved critical security/privacy issues.
- Valid measurement.
- Operational ownership.
- Documented product learnings.
- A clear set of next roadmap decisions.

No specific numeric threshold should be treated as universally valid; targets should be agreed for the actual pilot context.
