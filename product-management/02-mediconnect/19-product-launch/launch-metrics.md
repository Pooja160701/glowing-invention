# Launch Metrics

## North Star
**Completed Care Journey Rate (CCJR)**

## Launch KPIs

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Booking success rate | Validate transaction reliability |
| Search-to-book conversion | Identify discovery/booking friction |
| Availability success | Validate schedule visibility |
| Slot mismatch rate | Protect booking integrity |
| Self-service change completion | Measure appointment management |
| Notification delivery rate | Validate communication |
| Exception resolution time | Measure operational recovery |
| Core scheduling availability | Monitor reliability |
| Support volume | Detect operational friction |
| Critical incidents | Protect launch safety |

## Launch monitoring cadence

### First 24 hours
Monitor critical reliability and booking metrics closely.

### First week
Review:
- Funnel movement.
- Support themes.
- Exceptions.
- Notification reliability.
- Security events.
- Data quality.

### First month
Conduct a product review covering:
- KPI trends.
- Pilot feedback.
- Product gaps.
- Operational burden.
- Roadmap implications.

## Decision framework

Metrics are evidence for product decisions, not automatic success criteria. Interpret them against the predefined cohort, baseline, data quality, and operational context.
