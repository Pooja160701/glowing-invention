# MediConnect — Product Launch

**Status:** Portfolio-ready specification

This layer defines a controlled launch plan for the MediConnect administrative appointment platform.

## Launch objective

Validate that MediConnect can reliably support the core administrative journey:

**Find care → Book care → Prepare → Attend → Follow up**

The initial launch is intentionally controlled rather than a broad public rollout.

## Launch principles

- Protect appointment integrity.
- Minimize operational disruption.
- Validate measurement before scaling.
- Provide clear support ownership.
- Treat privacy and security as launch gates.
- Use pilot evidence to inform expansion.

## Launch scope

### Included
- Provider/clinic discovery.
- Availability.
- Booking.
- Confirmation.
- Reminders.
- Cancellation.
- Rescheduling.
- Staff scheduling operations.
- Exception handling.
- RBAC and audit logging.
- Core analytics.

### Excluded
- Diagnosis.
- Treatment recommendations.
- Prescribing.
- Emergency triage.
- Clinical decision support.
