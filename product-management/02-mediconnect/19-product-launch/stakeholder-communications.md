# Stakeholder Communications

## Internal launch announcement

**Subject:** MediConnect controlled MVP launch

MediConnect is entering a controlled MVP phase focused on reliable administrative appointment workflows.

The launch scope includes discovery, availability, booking, appointment management, reminders, staff operations, exception handling, security controls, and product analytics.

Teams should use the launch workspace for readiness status, incidents, metrics, and decisions.

## Pilot organization announcement

**Subject:** MediConnect pilot readiness

The pilot will validate appointment access and administrative scheduling workflows with a controlled group.

Pilot participants should know:
- What workflows are included.
- What support channel to use.
- How issues are escalated.
- What feedback is requested.
- What data is collected for product measurement.
- What workflows remain outside scope.

## Launch-day status update

Report:
- Launch status.
- Appointment activity.
- Booking success.
- Notification delivery.
- Open critical exceptions.
- Service reliability.
- Incidents.
- Support volume.

## Escalation communication

Every critical issue should include:
- What happened.
- Impact.
- Affected workflow.
- Current mitigation.
- Owner.
- Next update time.
