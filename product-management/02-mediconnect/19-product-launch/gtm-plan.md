# Go-to-Market Plan

## Target segment

Initial target hypothesis:
Independent clinics and small-to-mid-sized provider organizations with meaningful appointment volume and fragmented scheduling workflows.

## Buyer / stakeholder groups

### Economic buyer
Clinic or provider organization leadership.

### Operational buyer
Clinic operations or scheduling leadership.

### Daily users
- Patients.
- Clinic coordinators.
- Providers.

## Value proposition

MediConnect aims to make administrative appointment access easier for patients while giving clinics stronger scheduling visibility and exception handling.

## Messaging pillars

1. Easier appointment access.
2. More reliable scheduling.
3. Better self-service.
4. Clearer communication.
5. Operational visibility.
6. Privacy-aware product analytics.

## Pilot acquisition channels

- Direct provider outreach.
- Existing clinic relationships.
- Product demonstrations.
- Operational workflow assessments.
- Referral partnerships.

## Sales enablement

Prepare:
- Product overview.
- Workflow demo.
- Security/privacy overview.
- Pilot proposal.
- FAQ.
- Implementation checklist.
- Measurement plan.

## Pricing hypothesis

B2B2C SaaS pricing should be tested with pilot organizations.

Potential pricing dimensions:
- Organization size.
- Appointment volume.
- Provider count.
- Feature tier.

These are hypotheses requiring validation.
