# MVP Scope & Non-Goals

## In scope

### Patient
- Authentication.
- Provider/clinic discovery.
- Availability search.
- Appointment booking.
- Confirmation.
- Appointment history.
- Reminders.
- Cancellation.
- Rescheduling.
- Communication preferences.

### Staff
- Authentication/RBAC.
- Provider availability.
- Appointment management.
- Exception queue.
- Notification status.
- Basic operational analytics.

### Platform
- Audit logging.
- Event instrumentation.
- Security foundations.
- Privacy controls.
- Observability.
- API contracts.

## P1 / post-MVP

- External scheduling/EHR integrations.
- Advanced scheduling rules.
- Secure administrative messaging.
- More detailed analytics.
- Multi-location enterprise controls.
- Expanded patient intake.

## Explicit non-goals

MediConnect MVP will not provide:

- Diagnosis.
- Treatment recommendations.
- Prescription generation.
- Clinical decision support.
- Emergency triage.
- Medical advice.
- Clinical outcome prediction.
- EHR replacement.
- Comprehensive longitudinal medical-record management.
- Medical billing platform.
- Pharmacy marketplace.

## Why this boundary matters

Keeping the MVP administrative reduces product complexity, limits unnecessary sensitive-data collection, and makes the initial value proposition measurable around appointment access and operational efficiency.
