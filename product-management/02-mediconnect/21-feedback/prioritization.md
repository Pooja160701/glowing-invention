# Feedback Prioritization

## Recommended factors

Score each validated insight across:

- User impact.
- Frequency.
- Business/operational impact.
- Strategic alignment.
- Evidence confidence.
- Implementation effort.
- Risk reduction.

## Illustrative formula

**Insight Priority = (Impact × Frequency × Confidence × Strategic Alignment) ÷ Effort**

This is a portfolio planning heuristic, not a scientific measurement.

## Priority bands

### Immediate investigation
Use for:
- Security/privacy risks.
- Booking integrity issues.
- Critical reliability failures.
- Material operational failures.

### Near-term opportunity
Use when:
- Repeated evidence exists.
- Meaningful user friction is observed.
- Product strategy supports the opportunity.

### Research needed
Use when:
- Evidence is weak.
- Feedback conflicts with behavioral data.
- The opportunity is poorly understood.

### Monitor
Use for:
- Low-frequency requests.
- Low-impact feedback.
- Requests outside current strategy.

## Anti-bias rule

Do not prioritize solely because:
- A stakeholder is senior.
- A request is repeated by one loud customer.
- A feature is technically interesting.
- A feedback item is recent.
