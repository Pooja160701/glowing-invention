# Feedback Governance

## Data quality

Every feedback item should have:
- Source.
- Persona.
- Category.
- Type.
- Timestamp.
- Evidence confidence.
- Product area.

## Privacy

Do not collect unnecessary:
- Clinical details.
- Diagnoses.
- Treatment information.
- Medical record content.
- Message bodies.
- Direct identifiers where not required.

## Traceability

A validated insight should be traceable to:
Feedback → Research/Analytics evidence → Decision → Backlog/Experiment → Metric → Outcome.

## Synthetic data

All example feedback in this folder is synthetic and exists only to demonstrate the product-management workflow.

## Success criteria

The feedback system is successful when the team can identify recurring problems, distinguish signals from isolated requests, and convert validated insights into measurable product decisions.
