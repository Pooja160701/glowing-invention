# Customer Survey Framework

## Survey principles

- Keep surveys short.
- Ask one concept at a time.
- Avoid collecting clinical information.
- Separate satisfaction from product outcome.
- Analyze by relevant persona and workflow.
- Define action thresholds before reviewing results.

## CSAT

Question:
**How satisfied were you with this appointment-management experience?**

Scale:
1–5

Use after:
- Booking.
- Rescheduling.
- Cancellation.
- Support interaction.

## CES

Question:
**How easy was it to complete this task?**

Scale:
1–7

Use for:
- Finding availability.
- Booking.
- Rescheduling.
- Managing reminders.

## NPS

Question:
**How likely are you to recommend MediConnect for managing appointments?**

Scale:
0–10

Use sparingly for broader relationship tracking.

## Open-text prompt

**What was the most difficult part of completing your task?**

Do not request diagnosis, treatment, or other unnecessary clinical information.

## Survey metadata

Store:
- Survey ID.
- Persona.
- Workflow.
- Product version.
- Organization segment.
- Timestamp.
- Survey response.
- Optional structured reason.

Avoid direct identifiers unless required and approved.
