# Feedback Synthesis

## Weekly synthesis

### Step 1 — Normalize
Remove duplicates and classify feedback using the taxonomy.

### Step 2 — Cluster
Group feedback by:
- Workflow.
- Persona.
- Theme.
- Severity.
- Evidence confidence.

### Step 3 — Quantify
Track:
- Number of reports.
- Organizations affected.
- Users affected.
- Support volume.
- Related behavioral metric.

### Step 4 — Triangulate
Compare qualitative feedback with:
- Funnel metrics.
- Experiment results.
- Support data.
- Operational exceptions.
- Research findings.

### Step 5 — Form insight
Use:

**Observation → Evidence → Interpretation → Product implication**

### Example

**Observation:** Patients report uncertainty about whether availability is current.

**Evidence:** Repeated synthetic interviews plus availability-to-book funnel behavior.

**Interpretation:** Confidence may be a conversion barrier when availability freshness is unclear.

**Product implication:** Test availability freshness messaging while monitoring slot mismatch.

## Confidence

High confidence requires multiple evidence sources or repeated signals. A single request should not automatically become roadmap priority.
