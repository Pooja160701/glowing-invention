# Feedback Taxonomy

## Category

### Access
- Provider discovery.
- Availability.
- Search.
- Booking.

### Appointment management
- Cancellation.
- Rescheduling.
- History.
- Confirmation.

### Communications
- Reminders.
- Preferences.
- Delivery.
- Message clarity.

### Operations
- Staff workflow.
- Schedule management.
- Exceptions.
- Reporting.

### Trust
- Privacy.
- Security.
- Authorization.
- Transparency.

### Experience
- Usability.
- Accessibility.
- Navigation.
- Performance.

## Feedback type

- Feature request.
- Usability issue.
- Bug report.
- Reliability issue.
- Operational pain.
- Confusion.
- Positive signal.
- Complaint.
- Risk signal.
- Research question.

## Severity

- Critical.
- High.
- Medium.
- Low.

Severity should reflect user/business/security impact, not emotional intensity.

## Persona

- Patient.
- Clinic staff.
- Provider.
- Organization administrator.
- Support.

## Evidence confidence

- Direct observation.
- Repeated qualitative signal.
- Support trend.
- Behavioral data.
- Single request.
- Hypothesis.
