# Aha! Idea Management

## Idea intake sources

Ideas may originate from:
- Patient feedback.
- Clinic staff feedback.
- Provider feedback.
- Support tickets.
- Product analytics.
- Usability testing.
- Sales/customer discovery.
- Operational incidents.
- Competitive research.

## Idea template

### Title

Short problem-oriented title.

### Customer

Patient / Staff / Provider / Admin

### Problem

What problem is being experienced?

### Evidence

Research quote, observation, metric, support theme, or other evidence.

### Frequency

How often does the problem occur?

### Impact

What is the user/business/operational impact?

### Proposed outcome

What measurable change would indicate success?

### Related goal

Which product goal does this support?

### Dependencies

Technical, operational, security, privacy, or organizational dependencies.

### Status

New → Triaged → Under Research → Planned → Implemented → Rejected / Parked

---

## Example idea — synthetic

**Title:** Notify staff when provider availability changes affect booked appointments

**Customer:** Clinic Staff

**Problem:** Provider schedule changes can create appointment exceptions.

**Evidence:** Synthetic research identified schedule-conflict visibility as an opportunity.

**Outcome:** Reduce unresolved provider-change exceptions.

**Related goal:** Improve operational reliability.

**Status:** Planned hypothesis

**Validation:** Prototype workflow and pilot measurement before committing beyond MVP.
