# MediConnect Goals

## Goal 1 — Improve appointment access

**Outcome:** Patients can find and book appropriate appointment slots with less friction.

**Measures**
- Search-to-book conversion.
- Booking success rate.
- Slot mismatch rate.
- Time to first booking.

**Related initiatives**
- Reliable Appointment Access.
- Patient Discovery.

---

## Goal 2 — Reduce administrative appointment friction

**Outcome:** Routine changes require less staff-mediated coordination.

**Measures**
- Self-service change rate.
- Staff intervention rate.
- Reschedule completion rate.
- Exception volume.

**Related initiatives**
- Self-Service Patient Journey.
- Exception Operations.

---

## Goal 3 — Improve operational reliability

**Outcome:** Clinic teams can maintain schedules and recover from failures.

**Measures**
- Exception-resolution time.
- Scheduling conflict rate.
- Notification failure recovery.
- Availability update success.

**Related initiatives**
- Clinic Operational Control.

---

## Goal 4 — Establish trust and governance

**Outcome:** Patient and organization workflows use appropriate access, privacy, and audit controls.

**Measures**
- Authorization test coverage.
- Audit-event coverage.
- Privacy incidents.
- Sensitive analytics violations.

**Related initiatives**
- Trust, Security & Measurement.

---

## Goal 5 — Build a measurable product

**Outcome:** Product and operations teams can continuously understand the administrative care journey.

**Measures**
- Event coverage.
- Funnel completeness.
- KPI freshness.
- Reliability telemetry coverage.

**Related initiatives**
- Product Analytics & Observability.
