# MediConnect Initiatives

## I-01 — Reliable Appointment Access

**Goal:** Improve appointment access.

**Outcome:** Patients can reliably discover and book valid appointments.

**Key features**
- Provider discovery.
- Availability.
- Slot validation.
- Booking.
- Confirmation.
- Duplicate protection.

**Release:** 0.2–0.3

---

## I-02 — Self-Service Patient Journey

**Goal:** Reduce administrative appointment friction.

**Outcome:** Patients can manage eligible appointments independently.

**Key features**
- Appointment details.
- Cancellation.
- Rescheduling.
- History.
- Reminder preferences.

**Release:** 0.4

---

## I-03 — Clinic Operational Control

**Goal:** Improve operational reliability.

**Outcome:** Staff can maintain schedules and manage appointments safely.

**Key features**
- Staff schedule.
- Availability management.
- Administrative appointment updates.
- Organization scope.

**Release:** 0.3–0.5

---

## I-04 — Exception Operations

**Goal:** Reduce administrative failure recovery time.

**Outcome:** Staff can identify, assign, and resolve operational exceptions.

**Key features**
- Exception creation.
- Exception queue.
- Assignment.
- Resolution.
- Notification failure workflow.

**Release:** 0.5

---

## I-05 — Trust & Governance

**Goal:** Establish trust and governance.

**Outcome:** Access and sensitive administrative activity are controlled and auditable.

**Key features**
- RBAC.
- Organization isolation.
- Audit logs.
- Privacy controls.
- Analytics data minimization.

**Release:** 0.5

---

## I-06 — Product Analytics & Observability

**Goal:** Build a measurable product.

**Outcome:** Product, operations, and engineering teams can monitor the journey.

**Key features**
- Canonical event taxonomy.
- Booking funnel.
- Operational KPIs.
- Reliability telemetry.

**Release:** 0.3–1.0

---

## I-07 — Workflow Expansion

**Goal:** Extend product value after MVP validation.

**Outcome:** Support validated complex scheduling and external-system workflows.

**Key features**
- Advanced scheduling.
- External integrations.

**Release:** Post-1.0
