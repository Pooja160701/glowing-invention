# MediConnect — Strategic Risks & Assumptions

| ID | Assumption / Risk | Why It Matters | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-001 | Patients prefer self-service scheduling when availability is clear | Determines adoption | Patient research |
| A-002 | Clinics can provide sufficiently accurate availability | Determines booking reliability | Staff research + integration analysis |
| A-003 | Reminder delivery reduces avoidable missed appointments | Determines engagement value | Controlled measurement |
| A-004 | Staff can adopt a new scheduling workflow | Determines B2B feasibility | Usability testing |
| A-005 | Privacy controls are understandable to users | Determines trust | Research + usability testing |
| R-001 | Regional healthcare regulations differ | Compliance risk | Legal/compliance review |
| R-002 | Scheduling integrations may be heterogeneous | Technical risk | Integration discovery |
| R-003 | Notification channels may fail | Reliability risk | Delivery monitoring + fallback design |
| R-004 | Healthcare data is sensitive | Security risk | Threat modeling + security review |
