# MediConnect — Product Strategy

## Problem Space
Healthcare appointment journeys often involve disconnected discovery, scheduling, reminders, preparation information, and follow-up communication. Providers also need dependable schedule management and communication tools.

## Target Users

### Primary — Patients
People seeking outpatient appointments who need clear availability, booking confirmation, reminders, and appointment information.

### Secondary — Clinic Staff
Administrative teams responsible for schedules, appointment changes, cancellations, and patient communication.

### Secondary — Providers
Clinicians who need accurate appointment context and manageable schedule workflows.

## Strategic Pillars

### 1. Frictionless Access
Reduce steps and uncertainty in finding and booking appointments.

### 2. Reliable Scheduling
Prevent avoidable conflicts and provide clear appointment status.

### 3. Patient Engagement
Deliver timely, understandable administrative reminders and preparation information.

### 4. Trust & Privacy
Minimize sensitive data exposure and make consent, access, and communication preferences understandable.

### 5. Operational Efficiency
Reduce avoidable administrative work for clinic teams.

## North Star Outcome
**Completed Care Journey Rate (CCJR)** — percentage of eligible booked appointments that reach the defined completed-care administrative outcome without an avoidable scheduling or communication failure.

The metric is intentionally administrative/product-oriented and does not measure clinical outcomes.

## Supporting Outcomes
- Search-to-book conversion
- Booking completion rate
- Cancellation/no-show rate
- Reminder engagement
- Appointment change success rate
- Patient support contact rate
- Staff scheduling efficiency
- Privacy/security incident rate
