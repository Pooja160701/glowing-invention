# MediConnect — MVP Scope

## MVP Objective
Validate a reliable patient appointment journey and a practical provider scheduling workflow.

## P0 — MVP

### Patient
- Account creation/sign-in
- Provider/clinic discovery
- Appointment availability search
- Appointment booking
- Booking confirmation
- Appointment reminders
- Reschedule/cancel workflow
- Appointment history
- Communication preferences

### Clinic Staff
- Schedule management
- Appointment list
- Availability configuration
- Reschedule/cancellation handling
- Patient communication status

### Platform
- Role-based access
- Audit logging
- Notification delivery tracking
- Basic operational analytics
- Privacy controls

## P1 — Post-MVP
- Waitlist
- Two-way messaging
- Multi-location support
- Calendar integrations
- Advanced staff reporting
- Patient feedback workflows

## Explicit Non-Goals
- Diagnosis
- Clinical decision support
- Medication prescribing
- Medical emergency triage
- Automated clinical recommendations
- Replacement for clinician judgment

## MVP Success
The MVP should demonstrate that patients can reliably complete booking and appointment-management tasks while clinic staff can maintain schedules without introducing avoidable conflicts.
