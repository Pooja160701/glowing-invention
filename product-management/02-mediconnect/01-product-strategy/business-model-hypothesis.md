# MediConnect — Business Model Hypothesis

> Portfolio hypothesis; not a claim about an operating healthcare business.

## Potential Model
B2B2C SaaS where clinics or provider organizations pay for scheduling and engagement capabilities, while patients access core appointment workflows without a separate product fee.

## Potential Value Drivers
- Reduced administrative scheduling effort
- Improved appointment utilization
- Lower avoidable communication workload
- Better patient experience
- Operational visibility

## Business Metrics
- Clinic activation
- Active clinics
- Appointments booked per clinic
- Staff workflow adoption
- Platform retention
- Revenue per clinic
- Support cost per clinic

## Constraints
Healthcare economics, reimbursement models, privacy obligations, procurement requirements, and regional regulations can materially change the business model. These should be validated for the target market before commercialization.
