# MediConnect — Product Strategy

> **Project:** MediConnect — Healthcare Appointment & Patient Engagement Platform
>
> **Status:** Portfolio product strategy; healthcare workflows and metrics are proposed specifications, not a live healthcare service.

## Product Lifecycle
Discovery → Problem Definition → Research → Strategy → PRD → Design → Technical Planning → Analytics → Launch → Iteration

## Product Goal
Make it easier for patients to discover appropriate care, book appointments, receive useful reminders, and manage follow-up while giving healthcare providers a reliable workflow for appointment operations and patient communication.

## Important Boundary
MediConnect is an appointment and engagement product concept. It is not a diagnostic system and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
