# MediConnect — Patient & Provider Journey

## Patient Journey

### 1. Discover
Patient identifies a healthcare service or provider.

### 2. Evaluate
Patient reviews available administrative information such as specialty, location, availability, and appointment type.

### 3. Book
Patient selects an available slot and confirms required information.

### 4. Prepare
Patient receives appointment details, reminders, and administrative preparation instructions.

### 5. Attend
Appointment status is updated by the operational workflow.

### 6. Follow Up
Patient can review appointment history and manage future appointments.

## Provider/Staff Journey

### 1. Configure
Staff defines provider availability and appointment rules.

### 2. Monitor
Staff reviews upcoming appointments and exceptions.

### 3. Communicate
Staff handles permitted administrative notifications.

### 4. Resolve
Staff processes cancellations, reschedules, and schedule conflicts.

### 5. Review
Staff reviews operational metrics and recurring workflow problems.

## Opportunity Areas
- Reduce booking uncertainty.
- Make changes easier than abandoning an appointment.
- Improve reminder reliability.
- Give staff clear exception handling.
- Keep privacy boundaries visible and enforceable.
