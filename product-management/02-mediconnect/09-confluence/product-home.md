# MediConnect Product Home

## Product summary

MediConnect is a healthcare appointment access and patient engagement platform focused on administrative care-journey friction.

**Core journey:** Discover → Availability → Book → Confirm → Remind → Manage → Resolve exceptions.

## Product outcome

Enable patients to independently manage eligible appointment workflows while giving provider organizations reliable scheduling and operational control.

## North Star

**Completed Care Journey Rate (CCJR)**

Percentage of eligible booked appointments reaching the defined administrative completion state without avoidable scheduling or communication failure.

## Personas

- Time-Constrained Patient
- Clinic Coordinator
- Provider Stakeholder

## Current roadmap

### Now
Reliable appointment discovery and booking.

### Next
Self-service appointment management and operational exception handling.

### Later
Advanced scheduling, messaging, integrations, and enterprise workflows.

## Product boundaries

### In scope
- Appointment discovery.
- Scheduling.
- Booking.
- Reminders.
- Cancellation/rescheduling.
- Staff operations.
- Privacy/security foundations.
- Product/operational analytics.

### Out of scope
- Diagnosis.
- Treatment recommendations.
- Prescribing.
- Emergency triage.
- Clinical decision support.
- EHR replacement.
