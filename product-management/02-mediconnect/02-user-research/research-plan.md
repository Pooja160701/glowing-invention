# MediConnect — Research Plan

## Objectives
### Patient
- Identify booking friction.
- Understand reminder preferences.
- Identify cancellation/rescheduling pain points.
- Understand trust and privacy expectations.

### Clinic Staff
- Understand schedule management workflows.
- Identify manual administrative work.
- Understand conflict-resolution workflows.
- Identify operational reporting gaps.

## Proposed Sample
For an actual discovery study:
- 8–12 patients
- 5–8 clinic staff
- 3–5 provider stakeholders

For this portfolio, the attached participant dataset is synthetic.

## Research Approach
1. Recruit representative participants.
2. Conduct 30–45 minute interviews.
3. Capture direct observations and quotes.
4. Tag findings by theme.
5. Separate observations from interpretations.
6. Identify repeated problems.
7. Validate high-impact assumptions with follow-up research.

## Ethics & Privacy
Do not collect unnecessary health information. Avoid real medical records or sensitive clinical details in product discovery artifacts.
