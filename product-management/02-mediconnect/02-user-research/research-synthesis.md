# MediConnect — Research Synthesis

## Theme 1 — Availability Confidence
Patients and providers both need confidence that displayed availability is current.

**Implication:** Availability correctness is foundational, not a secondary UX enhancement.

## Theme 2 — Self-Service Changes
Both patients and staff experience friction around cancellations and rescheduling.

**Implication:** Change workflows should be first-class product flows.

## Theme 3 — Communication Control
Patients want reminders but differ in preferred timing and channel. Staff need visibility into delivery status.

**Implication:** Preferences and delivery observability belong in the MVP design.

## Theme 4 — Administrative Efficiency
Staff spend time on repetitive coordination and exception handling.

**Implication:** Staff workflows should optimize exception resolution rather than simply digitize existing manual work.

## Theme 5 — Privacy & Trust
Users want to understand what information is collected and how it is used.

**Implication:** Privacy explanations should appear at relevant moments, not only in a legal document.

## Theme 6 — Healthcare Boundary
Administrative scheduling products must not blur into diagnosis or treatment recommendations.

**Implication:** Product language, UX, analytics, and technical requirements should reinforce the boundary.
