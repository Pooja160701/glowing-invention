# MediConnect — User Research

> **Important:** The participant data in this folder is **synthetic portfolio data** created for product-management practice. It is not real patient data and must not be presented as real healthcare research.

## Research Goal
Understand administrative friction in appointment discovery, booking, changes, reminders, and clinic scheduling.

## Research Audiences
- Patients
- Clinic front-desk / scheduling staff
- Providers as workflow stakeholders

## Research Questions
1. How do patients currently find and book appointments?
2. Where does booking friction occur?
3. What causes patients to abandon or delay booking?
4. How do patients manage appointment changes?
5. Which reminders are useful versus intrusive?
6. What creates trust in a healthcare digital product?
7. What scheduling problems consume staff time?
8. Which exceptions are hardest for staff to manage?
9. What information does staff need to resolve an appointment issue?
10. What privacy/access concerns affect adoption?

## Methods
- Semi-structured interviews
- Journey mapping
- Jobs-to-be-done analysis
- Thematic synthesis
- Usability testing in later design stages

## Research Boundary
Research focuses on administrative/product experiences, not clinical diagnosis or treatment decisions.
