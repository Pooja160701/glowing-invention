# MediConnect — Interview Guide

## Patient Interview

### Context
- Tell me about the last time you needed an appointment.
- How did you find the provider or clinic?
- What information did you need before booking?

### Booking
- Walk me through how you booked.
- Where did you feel uncertain?
- What made you trust the booking confirmation?

### Changes
- Have you ever needed to reschedule or cancel?
- What did you have to do?
- What made that process difficult?

### Reminders
- Which reminders are useful?
- Which channels do you prefer?
- When do reminders become annoying?

### Trust
- What would make you hesitant to use a healthcare scheduling app?
- What privacy information would you want to see?

## Clinic Staff Interview

### Workflow
- Walk me through a typical appointment-booking day.
- What causes the most schedule changes?
- Which tasks are repetitive?

### Exceptions
- How do you handle cancellations?
- How do you handle double-booking or availability conflicts?
- What happens when a patient misses an appointment?

### Communication
- How are reminders currently sent?
- How do you know whether communication succeeded?

### Operations
- What reports do you need?
- Which workflow would you most like to automate?

## Closing
- If you could change one part of the appointment journey, what would it be?
- What would make you trust a new system?
