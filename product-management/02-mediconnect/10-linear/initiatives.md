# MediConnect Linear Initiatives

## INIT-01 — Reliable Appointment Access

**Outcome:** Patients can discover and book valid appointment slots with minimal avoidable failure.

**Success measures**
- Booking success rate.
- Search-to-book conversion.
- Slot mismatch rate.
- Duplicate booking rate.

**Projects**
- Patient Discovery.
- Availability & Booking.

---

## INIT-02 — Self-Service Patient Journey

**Outcome:** Patients can manage eligible appointments without unnecessary staff intervention.

**Success measures**
- Self-service change rate.
- Reschedule completion rate.
- Staff intervention rate.
- Reminder delivery rate.

**Projects**
- Appointment Management.
- Patient Communications.

---

## INIT-03 — Clinic Operational Control

**Outcome:** Clinic teams can manage schedules and recover from appointment exceptions efficiently.

**Success measures**
- Exception-resolution time.
- Exceptions per 100 appointments.
- Schedule update success.
- Notification recovery rate.

**Projects**
- Staff Scheduling.
- Exception Operations.

---

## INIT-04 — Trust, Security & Measurement

**Outcome:** MediConnect can operate with controlled access, auditability, privacy-safe analytics, and measurable reliability.

**Success measures**
- Authorization test coverage.
- Audit-event coverage.
- Sensitive analytics violations.
- Critical service availability.
- P95 scheduling latency.

**Projects**
- Security & Governance.
- Product Analytics & Observability.

---

## INIT-05 — Workflow Expansion

**Outcome:** Expand MediConnect after MVP evidence validates additional workflow demand.

**Success measures**
- Validated adoption of new workflows.
- Incremental administrative value.
- Integration reliability.

**Projects**
- Advanced Scheduling.
- External Integrations.
