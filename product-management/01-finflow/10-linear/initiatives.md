# FinFlow — Linear Initiatives

## INIT-01 — Financial Clarity
**Objective:** Help users move from raw transactions to clear spending understanding.
**Projects:** Transaction Foundation; Financial Dashboard
**Outcome metrics:** Import completion, dashboard activation, categorization correction rate.

## INIT-02 — Budget & Goal Management
**Objective:** Help users plan spending and monitor financial goals.
**Projects:** Budget & Goals
**Outcome metrics:** Budget creation, budget monitoring, goal creation, goal monitoring.

## INIT-03 — Actionable Financial Insights
**Objective:** Turn financial patterns into explainable, relevant actions.
**Projects:** Insights & Engagement
**Outcome metrics:** Insight engagement, insight action, insight dismissal.

## INIT-04 — Trust, Privacy & Measurement
**Objective:** Make the product measurable and trustworthy.
**Projects:** Analytics & Trust
**Outcome metrics:** Event coverage, notification opt-out, privacy-control usage, critical privacy/security defects.
