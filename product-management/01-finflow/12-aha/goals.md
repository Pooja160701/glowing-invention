# FinFlow — Aha! Goals

## GOAL-01 — Improve Financial Understanding

**Goal:** Help users understand their financial position with less effort.

### Success Signals
- Higher dashboard activation
- Higher transaction review completion
- Lower time-to-understanding in usability testing

### Initiatives
- Financial Data Foundation
- Financial Clarity Dashboard

---

## GOAL-02 — Improve Financial Planning

**Goal:** Help users establish and monitor spending and savings plans.

### Success Signals
- Budget creation
- Budget monitoring
- Goal creation
- Goal monitoring

### Initiatives
- Budget Control
- Savings Goals

---

## GOAL-03 — Increase Actionability

**Goal:** Help users identify and act on meaningful financial patterns.

### Success Signals
- Insight engagement
- Insight action rate
- Insight dismissal rate

### Initiative
- Explainable Financial Insights

---

## GOAL-04 — Build Trust & Sustainable Engagement

**Goal:** Make financial-data usage transparent and product communication relevant.

### Success Signals
- Privacy-control usage
- Notification opt-out rate
- Critical privacy/security defects
- User feedback

### Initiatives
- Privacy & Data Controls
- Notification Management
