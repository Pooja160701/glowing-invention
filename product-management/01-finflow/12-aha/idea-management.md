## Idea Review Questions

1. What user problem does this solve?
2. What evidence supports the problem?
3. Which product goal does it support?
4. What outcome could improve?
5. What is the expected effort?
6. What risks exist?
7. How will success be measured?

Ideas should not become roadmap commitments without sufficient evidence and product fit.
