# FinFlow — Go-to-Market Plan

## Target Audience
Initial target segments:
- Early-career professionals
- Budget-conscious professionals
- Goal-oriented savers

## Positioning Hypothesis
A financial wellness product focused on turning financial information into understandable planning and action.

## Acquisition Channels
- Product landing page
- Organic search/content
- Community partnerships
- Referral loop
- Professional/social content

## Launch Phases

### Phase 0 — Internal Validation
Validate core journeys and instrumentation.

### Phase 1 — Controlled Cohort
Invite a small target-user cohort and closely monitor errors and feedback.

### Phase 2 — Broader Availability
Expand when product quality and activation evidence meet predefined release criteria.

### Phase 3 — Optimization
Use behavioral data and research to improve activation, engagement, retention, and trust.

## Messaging Principles
- Explain value clearly.
- Avoid unrealistic financial promises.
- Do not imply financial advice when the product provides educational/product insights.
- Explain data use and privacy controls.
