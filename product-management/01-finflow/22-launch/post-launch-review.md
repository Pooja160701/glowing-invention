# FinFlow — Post-Launch Product Review

## Review Timing
- Day 1 operational review
- Week 1 product review
- Day 30 outcome review

## Questions
### Customer
- Did users reach value?
- What blocked activation?
- What created trust or distrust?

### Product
- Which capabilities were actually used?
- Which workflow had the largest friction?

### Quality
- Were imports reliable?
- Were categorization corrections manageable?
- Were there data/privacy issues?

### Business
- Did acquisition convert to meaningful usage?
- What evidence supports continued investment?

## Output
1. Metrics summary
2. Customer evidence
3. Quality review
4. Key decisions
5. Updated opportunities
6. Roadmap changes
