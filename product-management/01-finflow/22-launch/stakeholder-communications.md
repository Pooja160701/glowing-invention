# FinFlow — Stakeholder Communication Plan

## Audience: Product & Engineering
**Cadence:** Weekly during launch preparation

Topics:
- Scope
- Delivery status
- Risks
- Quality
- Metrics readiness
- Launch blockers

## Audience: Leadership
**Cadence:** Milestone-based

Topics:
- Product outcome
- Launch readiness
- Major risks
- Expected learning goals

## Audience: Support / Operations
**Cadence:** Before launch + daily during controlled launch

Topics:
- Product behavior
- Known issues
- FAQ
- Escalation paths

## Audience: Early Users
Communicate:
- What is available
- What the product does not yet support
- Privacy/data controls
- How to submit feedback

## Communication Rule
Separate confirmed facts, observed metrics, hypotheses, and planned work in all launch communications.
