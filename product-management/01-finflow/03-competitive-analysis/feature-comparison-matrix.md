## Product Interpretation

The table suggests that basic aggregation, categorization, budgeting, goals, and dashboards are crowded capabilities.

FinFlow's product differentiation should therefore be expressed through the **quality of the decision-support experience**, not through feature-count competition.

### Proposed Differentiation

**"Understand your money, know what matters, decide what to do next."**

This is a portfolio positioning hypothesis that should be validated through usability testing and product experiments.
