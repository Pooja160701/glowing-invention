# FinFlow — Jobs-to-be-Done

## JTBD 1 — Understand Spending

**When** I review my finances after a busy period,  
**I want to** quickly understand where my money went,  
**so I can** identify what needs attention without manually analyzing every transaction.

## JTBD 2 — Stay Within Budget

**When** I am approaching a category limit,  
**I want to** know early enough to adjust my spending,  
**so I can** avoid ending the month significantly over budget.

## JTBD 3 — Maintain Financial Data

**When** new transactions arrive,  
**I want to** have them categorized with minimal manual effort,  
**so I can** keep my financial picture current.

## JTBD 4 — Make Progress Toward Goals

**When** I am saving for a specific goal,  
**I want to** see meaningful progress and understand how current behavior affects it,  
**so I can** stay motivated and adjust when necessary.

## JTBD 5 — Trust Financial Insights

**When** the product gives me a financial insight,  
**I want to** understand why it was generated,  
**so I can** decide whether the recommendation is relevant to me.
