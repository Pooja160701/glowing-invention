# FinFlow — User Personas

> **Status:** Personas are synthesized from the simulated research dataset and are not representations of real individuals.

## Persona 1 — The Emerging Professional

**Profile:** Early-career worker managing independent finances for the first time.

### Goals
- Understand monthly spending
- Build a stable emergency fund
- Avoid accidental overspending

### Frustrations
- Manual categorization
- Fragmented financial information
- Limited visibility into trends

### Needs
- Simple dashboard
- Automatic categorization with correction controls
- Clear budget progress

### Product Opportunity
Reduce the effort required to move from transactions to understanding.

---

## Persona 2 — The Structured Budgeter

**Profile:** User who already tracks finances but spends significant time maintaining the system.

### Goals
- Stay within category budgets
- Detect recurring expenses
- Improve savings consistency

### Frustrations
- Manual data entry
- Repetitive maintenance
- Weak alerts

### Needs
- Automated ingestion
- Budget alerts
- Recurring-expense detection

### Product Opportunity
Automate repetitive financial-management tasks without removing user control.

---

## Persona 3 — The Goal-Oriented Saver

**Profile:** User motivated by a concrete financial goal.

### Goals
- Reach a target amount
- Understand progress
- Connect daily spending with longer-term goals

### Frustrations
- Progress can feel disconnected from spending behavior
- Generic reminders

### Needs
- Goal tracking
- Progress visualization
- Contextual insights

### Product Opportunity
Connect everyday financial behavior to meaningful goals.
