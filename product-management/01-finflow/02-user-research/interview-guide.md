# FinFlow — Interview Guide

> **Status:** Simulated interview guide for portfolio demonstration.

## Introduction

"Thanks for speaking with us. We're exploring how people manage everyday finances. There are no right or wrong answers. We're interested in your current behavior and experiences."

## Section 1 — Current Behavior

1. How do you currently keep track of your spending?
2. When was the last time you reviewed your expenses?
3. What tools do you use?
4. What works well about your current approach?
5. What is frustrating about it?

## Section 2 — Budgeting

1. Do you currently have a monthly budget?
2. How do you decide how much to allocate to categories?
3. What usually causes you to go over budget?
4. How do you notice that you are overspending?
5. What do you do after noticing it?

## Section 3 — Savings Goals

1. Are you currently saving toward a specific goal?
2. How do you track progress?
3. What makes it difficult to stay consistent?
4. What kind of reminder would be useful?

## Section 4 — Financial Insights

1. What information would help you make better spending decisions?
2. What would make a financial insight useful rather than annoying?
3. Would you want an explanation for why an insight was shown?
4. Which actions would you want to take directly from an insight?

## Section 5 — Trust & Privacy

1. What concerns would you have about sharing financial data?
2. What information would you expect a finance app to explain clearly?
3. What controls would make you more comfortable?
4. Which notifications would feel intrusive?

## Closing

- What is the single biggest frustration in managing your finances?
- If one thing could be automated, what would it be?
- What would make you recommend a financial-management product?
