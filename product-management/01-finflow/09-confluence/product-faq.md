# FinFlow — Product FAQ

## What is FinFlow?
FinFlow is a personal finance and financial wellness product focused on helping users understand spending, manage budgets, track goals, and act on financial insights.

## Does FinFlow move money?
No. Autonomous money movement is explicitly outside the MVP scope.

## Does FinFlow provide regulated financial advice?
The MVP is designed to provide informational product-generated insights, not regulated financial advice.

## Can users correct transaction categories?
Yes. User correction is a core MVP requirement.

## Why are insights explainable?
Financial information is sensitive. Users should understand the context behind an insight before deciding whether to act.

## What happens if transaction data is incomplete?
The product should avoid presenting unsupported conclusions and should communicate data limitations.

## Is the research in this portfolio real?
The research dataset is synthetic and exists only to demonstrate a complete PM workflow.

## Are Jira and Confluence actually connected?
No. The Jira and Confluence sections are structured specifications/import-ready artifacts unless a real external workspace is connected.
