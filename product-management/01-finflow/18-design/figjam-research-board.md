# FinFlow — FigJam Research Synthesis Board

> Board specification for recreating in FigJam or Miro.

## Frame 1 — Research Questions
- How do users currently understand spending?
- Where does manual effort occur?
- What makes financial tools feel trustworthy?
- What causes users to abandon budgeting?

## Frame 2 — Affinity Themes
### Manual Effort
- Repetitive categorization
- Spreadsheet maintenance

### Visibility
- Unclear spending patterns
- Weak budget awareness

### Goals
- Difficulty tracking progress

### Trust
- Privacy concerns
- Need for explainable insights

## Frame 3 — Opportunity Areas
1. Reduce data-entry effort.
2. Improve financial visibility.
3. Make planning actionable.
4. Explain automated insights.
5. Give users control over notifications and data.

## Frame 4 — How Might We
- HMW reduce manual categorization without hiding control?
- HMW make financial status understandable at a glance?
- HMW help users act on insights safely?
