## Dashboard Interactions
Selecting a product version filters all KPI views. Selecting an acquisition source filters activation and retention. Selecting a lifecycle cohort updates action mix.

## Recommended Tableau Questions
- Which onboarding step has the largest drop-off?
- Does import completion differ by platform?
- Which financial actions contribute most to WAFAU?
- How does correction rate change after categorization releases?

All sample outputs should be marked synthetic until production data is available.
