# FinFlow — Product Vision

## Product
FinFlow is a personal finance and AI-assisted financial wellness platform designed to help consumers understand their money, build healthier financial habits, and make informed day-to-day financial decisions.

## Problem
Many consumers can access transaction data but struggle to turn it into clear, actionable financial decisions. Common problems include fragmented financial information, unclear spending patterns, difficulty maintaining budgets, and limited visibility into progress toward financial goals.

## Target Users
- Young professionals managing income and expenses independently
- Early-career users building their first structured budget
- Users who want better visibility into recurring spending and savings
- Users working toward short- and medium-term financial goals

## Vision Statement
Make personal financial management simple, understandable, proactive, and habit-forming.

## Product Principles
1. **Clarity over complexity** — present financial information in language users can understand.
2. **Action over reporting** — insights should lead to practical next steps.
3. **Trust by design** — privacy, transparency, and user control are core product requirements.
4. **Progress over perfection** — help users build sustainable financial habits.
5. **Explain recommendations** — users should understand why an insight or recommendation appears.

## Product Outcome
A successful FinFlow experience helps a user answer:
- Where did my money go?
- Am I on track with my budget?
- What recurring expenses should I review?
- How much can I safely allocate toward a goal?
- What action should I take next?

## MVP Scope
### In Scope
- Account/profile setup
- Transaction import
- Transaction categorization
- Spending dashboard
- Budget creation and tracking
- Savings goals
- Basic financial insights
- Notifications for meaningful budget events
- Product analytics instrumentation

### Out of Scope for MVP
- Direct bank transfers
- Lending or credit underwriting
- Investment execution
- Tax filing
- Personalized regulated financial advice
- Autonomous financial transactions

## Success Definition
The MVP should demonstrate that users can connect/import their financial data, understand their spending, establish a budget or goal, and return regularly to monitor progress.
