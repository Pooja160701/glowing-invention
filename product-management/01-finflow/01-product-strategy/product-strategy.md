# FinFlow — Product Strategy

> **Artifact status:** Portfolio specification. Research and business metrics in this project are synthetic unless explicitly marked otherwise.

## 1. Strategic Context

FinFlow addresses the gap between having access to personal financial data and being able to use that data effectively.

The product strategy focuses on reducing financial-management friction rather than maximizing the number of financial features.

## 2. Product Strategy

### North Star Outcome
**Users consistently understand their financial position and take at least one useful financial-management action during each active period.**

### Strategic Pillars

| Pillar | User Need | Product Response | Example Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financial clarity | Understand spending | Categorization + dashboard | Dashboard engagement |
| Budget control | Avoid overspending | Budget tracking + alerts | Budget adherence |
| Goal progress | Build savings habits | Goal tracking | Goal contribution rate |
| Actionable insights | Know what to do next | Contextual insights | Insight action rate |
| Trust | Feel safe using the product | Privacy controls + transparency | Trust/consent signals |

## 3. Primary Product Bet

If FinFlow reduces the effort required to understand spending and act on financial information, users will engage more consistently with budgeting and savings workflows.

## 4. MVP Prioritization

### Must Have
- Transaction ingestion/import
- Transaction categorization
- Spending overview
- Budget setup
- Budget progress
- Savings goals
- Basic insight generation
- Core analytics events

### Should Have
- Recurring-expense detection
- Custom categories
- Goal reminders
- Monthly financial summary

### Could Have
- Household/shared budgets
- Advanced forecasting
- Subscription optimization
- Scenario planning

### Not Now
- Investment execution
- Credit products
- Financial-product marketplace
- Autonomous money movement

## 5. Business Model Hypothesis

Potential future model:
- Free core financial-management experience
- Premium tier for advanced analytics, forecasting, and automation

This is a **business-model hypothesis**, not evidence of validated willingness to pay.

## 6. Key Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Users distrust financial-data handling | High | Clear consent, data controls, transparent policies |
| Categorization errors | Medium | Editable categories + feedback loop |
| Too many notifications | Medium | User controls + event prioritization |
| Low recurring engagement | High | Goal progress + useful monthly summaries |
| Insights feel generic | Medium | Contextual, explainable insights |

## 7. Decision Framework

Every proposed feature should answer:

**Problem → User → Requirement → Solution → Metric → Business Outcome**

Features that cannot establish this chain should not automatically enter the roadmap.
