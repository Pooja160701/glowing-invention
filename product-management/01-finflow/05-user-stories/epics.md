# FinFlow — Epics

## EPIC-01 Onboarding & Financial Setup
Help a new user establish the minimum information needed to use FinFlow.

## EPIC-02 Transaction Import & Categorization
Enable users to bring transaction data into FinFlow and maintain accurate categories.

## EPIC-03 Financial Dashboard
Give users a clear view of their current financial position.

## EPIC-04 Budget Management
Help users create, monitor, and adjust spending budgets.

## EPIC-05 Savings Goals
Help users define and monitor progress toward financial goals.

## EPIC-06 Financial Insights
Turn financial activity into understandable, explainable observations and actions.

## EPIC-07 Notifications
Deliver meaningful financial events without creating notification fatigue.

## EPIC-08 Product Analytics
Instrument core product behavior for measurement and experimentation.

## EPIC-09 Privacy, Security & Trust
Give users appropriate control and transparency over financial data.
